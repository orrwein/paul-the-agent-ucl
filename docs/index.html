2026/27 UEFA Champions League

Every match, modelled & scored.

A Dixon–Coles + Elo engine locks a scoreline before kickoff, then grades itself against reality. Thirty-six clubs, one table, two-legged knockouts — no hindsight, nothing edited after the whistle, and every change of mind on the record.

Live scorecard

How the model is doing across every graded fixture. A pick is exact when the scoreline lands on the nose, right winner when the outcome was called but not the score, and a miss otherwise.

The public dashboard leads with accuracy; the points table runs a private prediction game.

Getting sharper

Accuracy per round, and the running cumulative line. The model refits its calibration, form and ratings after every matchday — this is whether that buys anything.

The league table

One table, thirty-six clubs, eight matches each. Finish top eight and you skip straight to the round of 16; ninth to twenty-fourth go into a two-legged play-off; twenty-fifth and below go home.

Picks vs results

Every pick is written to the database before kickoff and never touched once the whistle goes. Bets stay changeable until kickoff, so Paul may revise one when late team news lands — every version is kept, and the ones that changed say so. Each row shows what Paul called against what actually happened.

Second thoughts

A bet can be changed until kickoff, so the honest question is whether changing it helps. Every version of every pick is kept; this scores what the first call would have earned against what the final call actually earned, using the same rules either way. If revising is losing points, it says so.

Knockout bracket

Every pairing is fixed by league position — only the slot inside each band is drawn. The better-placed club hosts the second leg, ties are settled on aggregate, and there is no away-goals rule.

Title race

Probabilities from Monte Carlo simulations of the real 144-fixture schedule and the bracket it feeds. Matches already played are forced, so these are live odds conditioned on what has actually happened.

Top scorer race

Live standings, plus Paul's re-projected pick — current goals, extended by each player's rate over the matches their club is still expected to play. ⚽ marks a penalty taker.

Futures

The two season-long bets, locked before matchday one and shown against Paul's current live favourite, so you can watch them hold or drift.

Behind the scenes

Paul isn't a lucky octopus. Every pick is an ensemble of opponent-aware signals fused into expected goals, then a probability matrix over every scoreline, then the pick that maximises that round's points.
